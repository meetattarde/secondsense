NO EXTRA SENSOR NEEDED · VERY ROUGH

Camera-only distance guess

Uses a typical size for recognized object types and an assumed camera view angle. The real distance can be much wider than the estimate, especially if an object is partly hidden or an unusual size.

The displayed range is an illustrative ½×–2× band, not a measured confidence interval. It must not guide walking decisions.

SUPPORTED PHONES ONLY

Center-view distance

An experimental phone depth sensor may provide an approximate range. Availability depends on your phone and browser.

No depth reading

Important: Camera recognition can miss or mislabel things. Camera-only distances are guesses based on typical object sizes and may be substantially wrong. Hardware depth readings are available only on supported phones and refer to a small area at screen center. Neither feature tells you that a path is clear. Keep using your cane, guide dog, or other mobility aid.
